# 💫 About Me:
- Hi, I'm Paresh.

I'm a Soft Dev who loves to learn, experiment and try out new things. That's probably the simplest way to describe me.

But I'm not just about work and technology.

I'm a big music lover. I listen to all kinds of songs, but I have a special place for Ghazals, Sufi and devotional music. Sometimes I listen to a song because of the lyrics, sometimes because of the feeling, and sometimes there is no particular reason — I just like it. I also love sharing music with people. If I find a song I really like, chances are I'll send it to someone. 😄

I love philosophy and reading about life, people, thoughts and different ways of looking at things. I'm a little emotional, a little spiritual and an innocent soul... hahaha, at least that's what I like to believe. :)

I love expressing myself, but I also love to listen. I enjoy talking about random things, having long conversations, gossiping, sharing thoughts and hearing what other people have to say. I think sometimes you learn more by simply listening to someone else's story.

I also enjoy reading random articles and discussions on Quora, X (Twitter) and Reddit. There is always something interesting to find — someone's experience, a different opinion, a crazy idea or just something that makes you think. You can find me there too.

I love travelling and exploring. Sometimes I have a destination in mind, and sometimes I just want to get on the road and see where it takes me. I like discovering new places, taking pictures, meeting people and collecting experiences along the way.

And yes, there is the Soft Dev side of me too. I like learning new things, experimenting, figuring out how things work and sometimes breaking things while trying to make them better. 😄

This website is basically my little space on the internet. A place where I can share my work, things I learn, music I like, places I visit, thoughts I have, stories I want to tell and random things I find interesting.

I'm still figuring things out, just like everyone else.

On a journey of self-improvement, sculpting a better version of myself with each step.

That's all from me... for now. :)
